Privacy Policy

Queora · Last updated: 12 November 2026

Queora is a download manager for Android. This policy explains what data the app handles and what it does not.

Data we do not collect

Queora does not require an account. In the current version we do not operate a Queora server that receives your download history, files, or browsing activity. We do not sell personal data.

Data stored on your device

  • Download list, progress, and settings (local database on the device)
  • Files you choose to download, in app storage or a folder you select
  • Optional preferences such as default connection count and Wi-Fi-only

Permissions

  • Internet — to download files you request
  • Notifications — to show transfer progress and completion
  • Storage / folder access — only when you pick a download folder or share a file

Contact

Privacy questions: use the contact method on the support page. Replace the support email with your real address before store submission.
